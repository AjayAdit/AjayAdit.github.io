// About Me

Engineering Intelligence into Motion

I'm currently pursuing my MS in Robotics at Northeastern University, where I focus on the intersection of perception, planning, and control systems.

My research interests center on Control Barrier Functions (CBFs) for safe autonomous systems, efficient motion planning algorithms, and simulation-based validation using platforms like Genesis.

I chose Northeastern for its strong robotics perception program, Boston's vibrant tech ecosystem, and the unique co-op opportunities that bridge academic research with industry applications.

Areas of Focus
